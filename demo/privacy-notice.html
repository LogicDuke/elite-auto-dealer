What we collect and why

You can browse and search our vehicles without giving us any personal data. We only receive the information you choose to send with the enquiry form, which asks for your name, your email address, a telephone number (optional), a message (optional) and your agreement to this notice. We use it for the purpose of the subject you choose:

  • General enquiry: to answer your question.
  • Vehicle enquiry (an enquiry sent from a vehicle page): as above, together with the vehicle you asked about. We keep the vehicle’s name and stock number with your enquiry so we still know which car you meant after it has been sold.
  • Test drive: to arrange a test drive with you.
  • Finance: to discuss financing options with you. Sending a finance enquiry is not a credit application. [If the dealership passes finance enquiries to a lender or credit intermediary, name them here and explain when this happens.]
  • Trade-in / valuation: to assess the vehicle you would like to trade in, from the details you write in your message (for example its make, model, year and mileage). This is private enquiry information: it is never published on this website, and the website does not let visitors list or sell vehicles.

With each enquiry we also store the subject, the date and time it was received, and a copy of the statement you agreed to. We do not store your IP address or browser details with your enquiry. If you call us or message us on WhatsApp through the links on this website, that conversation takes place outside this website.
