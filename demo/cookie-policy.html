Remove this paragraph once the policy describes a real business.

Cookies and browser storage

Cookies are small text files a website asks your browser to keep. Browsers also offer local storage and session storage, which work in a similar way but are not sent to the website with each request. This policy covers all three.

This website sets no cookies for visitors and uses no analytics, advertising, social-media tracking or other optional services. Every page, image and script is loaded from this website itself. Because nothing optional is used, there is nothing to accept or reject, and you will not be asked to choose.

Necessary

These items keep the website working or remember a choice you made. They contain nothing that identifies you and are not used to follow you.

  • eds-dps:elite-auto-dealer (local storage, demonstration sites only): remembers the colour palette you previewed with the “Try Colors” tool. It contains only the palette’s name, is never sent to the website, and is removed when you choose “Reset to Default”. It is a demonstration feature, not analytics or marketing.
  • wordpress_logged_in_*, wordpress_sec_*, wordpress_test_cookie and wp-settings-* (cookies, WordPress): set only for dealership staff who log in to manage the website. They keep staff signed in and remember their admin screen settings. Visitors who do not log in never receive them.

Sending the enquiry form does not create a cookie either. Just before sending, your browser fetches a fresh security token from this website so that the form also works on cached pages; the enquiry itself is handled as described in the [eda_detail field="privacy"].

If optional services are added

If the website later uses an optional service, such as maps, video or analytics, it stays switched off until you allow it. You will then be asked once, and you can change your mind at any time; your choice is kept in your browser under eds-consent:elite-auto-dealer (local storage) for 180 days, or until this policy changes in a way that needs a new choice. This item does not exist while no optional service is used.

Your choices

Cookie preferences, also in the footer of every page, shows what this website stores at any time. You can delete these items in your browser settings; the palette preview is also removed with “Reset to Default”.
