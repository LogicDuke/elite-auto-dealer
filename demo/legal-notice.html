Vehicle information

We describe every vehicle with care. Prices, equipment, technical specifications, mileage, registration date, photographs and availability are based on the information available to us when the listing was published and may change, for example when a vehicle is reserved or sold. Photographs illustrate the vehicle offered; colours can look different on screen and photographs may not show every detail, option or mark of use. Equipment lists summarise the main features and are not exhaustive.

A vehicle shown on this website is not a binding offer. The price, equipment, condition, mileage, availability and other terms of a purchase are confirmed by the dealership in writing (in the order form or contract) before you commit. If you notice an error in a listing, please tell us; obvious errors are corrected as soon as we become aware of them. None of this limits the rights you have as a consumer under Belgian and European law, including the legal guarantee of conformity.
